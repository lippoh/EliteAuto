🔷 Project Overview:
Goal:
Create a sleek, serious one-page website for importing luxury cars from Dubai to Europe (Greece & Bulgaria) — focused on high-performance, luxury, and SUV vehicles. The tone should be disciplined, ambitious, and trustworthy. The site must clearly communicate why we do this, how we do it, and who we are — with a strong focus on our mission to connect European buyers with smarter automotive value.
